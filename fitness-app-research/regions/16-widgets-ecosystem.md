# widgets-ecosystem

## Key findings
- Free provisioning (Personal Team) is confirmed viable for this app's core glanceability stack: Apple's official capabilities table shows HealthKit, App Groups, Background Modes, Data Protection, Keychain Sharing, and Maps all have checkmarks in the free 'Apple Developer' column; WidgetKit, Live Activities (local updates), App Intents/Shortcuts, and local notifications require no entitlement at all, so they also work when sideloaded.
- The hard free-provisioning exclusions are: Push Notifications (no APNs -> no push-started/push-updated Live Activities, no remote nudges), Time Sensitive Notifications entitlement, Communication Notifications, all iCloud/CloudKit, Siri capability (SiriKit), Wallet, and WeatherKit. Everything glanceable must therefore be driven locally on-device.
- Free provisioning operational constraints: apps expire after 7 days and must be re-deployed from Xcode weekly (when expired, the app won't launch and widgets render as blanks/placeholders), max ~3 sideloaded apps per device, and 10 App IDs per 7-day window per team — significant because this project needs ~4 bundle IDs (iOS app + iOS widget extension + watch app + watch widget extension), so bundle ID churn during setup can exhaust the weekly cap.
- Widget refresh budget: WidgetKit gives roughly 40-70 timeline reloads per day per widget (about every 15-60 min) over a rolling 24h window tuned to user habits; reloads are FREE (don't count against budget) when the containing app is foregrounded, during an active audio/navigation session, or when the widget executes an App Intent — so a fitness app that gets opened daily can effectively refresh its widgets on every app open via WidgetCenter.shared.reloadAllTimelines().
- HealthKit + widgets architecture constraint: health data is protected data, encrypted and unreadable while the iPhone is locked ('health data usually inaccessible while locked' per Apple docs), and widget extensions cannot request HealthKit authorization; therefore the reliable pattern is: main app queries HealthKit (woken by background delivery), writes a JSON/UserDefaults snapshot into an App Group container, then calls WidgetCenter reloadTimelines — the widget never queries HealthKit directly.
- HealthKit background delivery (com.apple.developer.healthkit.background-delivery, part of the HealthKit capability, iOS 15+) wakes the app when new samples arrive, but step count on iOS is throttled to at most hourly; observer queries must be registered in app launch code, and after 3 unacknowledged deliveries HealthKit stops sending updates.
- Live Activities are the single highest-value surface for workouts: iOS 16.1+, presentations = Lock Screen banner, Dynamic Island compact/minimal/expanded, StandBy, and (iOS 18/watchOS 11+) they appear AUTOMATICALLY in the Apple Watch Smart Stack with no watch app required — customizable via supplementalActivityFamilies([.small]) in the ActivityConfiguration.
- Live Activity constraints: 4KB max combined static+dynamic payload (including images, which must not exceed presentation size, e.g. ~45x36.67pt for minimal), 8-hour max active duration (then auto-ends; stays on Lock Screen up to 4 more hours), must be started while app is foreground (or via a LiveActivityIntent from iOS 17.2+), can be updated/ended from background, staleDate and relevanceScore control freshness display and Dynamic Island priority.
- Without APNs, Live Activity updates during a workout must come from local background runtime — two free-provisioning-compatible paths: (1) 'location' background mode during GPS workouts keeps the app alive to call Activity.update() every few seconds (Background Modes is free-tier); (2) watchOS HKWorkoutSession with startMirroringToCompanionDevice (watchOS 10/iOS 17+) mirrors the watch workout to the iPhone app with background runtime and bidirectional data (sendToRemoteWorkoutSession), letting the phone drive the Live Activity from watch heart-rate data.
- HKWorkoutSession is watchOS 2.0+ (the real-time sensor path: high-frequency heart rate, workout-tuned sensors, one session at a time) and only reached iOS/iPadOS at 17.0 — on iPhone it requires an external HR sensor for heart rate; for an Apple Watch Ultra 3 owner the workout session should run on the watch (native watch app) or use Apple's Workout app + read samples after the fact.
- Watch complications are now just WidgetKit accessory widgets: accessoryCircular/accessoryRectangular/accessoryInline (watchOS 9+, shared with iOS 16+ Lock Screen) plus watch-only accessoryCorner; one SwiftUI codebase serves iPhone Lock Screen widgets, watch face complications, and the watchOS Smart Stack. Complication presence on the active watch face grants the watch app a budget of ~4 background updates/hour (shared between HealthKit background delivery and WKApplicationRefreshBackgroundTask).
- Interactive widgets (iOS 17+) allow only Button and Toggle wired to App Intents — no navigation, no text input, inactive while the device is locked; AppIntent runs in the widget extension process by default, but LiveActivityIntent and ForegroundContinuableIntent run in the app's process; interactions trigger a guaranteed free timeline reload, and toggles render optimistically before perform() completes; use .invalidatableContent() on values awaiting confirmation.
- StandBy (iOS 17+) shows two systemSmall widgets side-by-side scaled up while iPhone charges horizontally; the widget's containerBackground(for: .widget) is removed by the system, Night Mode applies a monochrome red tint in low light, and Apple's guidance is to scale up text rather than rely on rich images/color — so the systemSmall widget must be designed background-removable and legible from across a room (also relevant: bedside sleep-tracking display).
- Lock Screen accessory widgets render in 'vibrant' mode (desaturated, tinted to wallpaper) and iOS 18+ Home Screen tinting uses 'accented' mode that strips widget colors and applies the user's tint — meaning the Anthropic-style cream/coral brand palette will be stripped on these surfaces; design must survive monochrome via shape/hierarchy (min 11pt text), with full brand color reliably visible only in default Home Screen mode, StandBy day mode, and the Live Activity (which keeps custom colors).
- App Intents (iOS 16+) requires no entitlement and powers Shortcuts, Spotlight, the iPhone Action button, the Apple Watch Ultra Action button, Focus filters, iOS 18+ Control Center/Lock Screen Controls (ControlWidget), and interactive widgets — so 'press Ultra 3 Action button -> start workout logging' and 'log water/meal via Shortcut' work fully under free provisioning; iOS 17.2+ LiveActivityIntent can even start a Live Activity without opening the app.
- Notifications for smart nudges must be local-only under free provisioning: UNUserNotificationCenter with calendar/interval triggers needs no entitlement and supports actionable buttons (e.g. 'Log it' action); the Time Sensitive entitlement is paid-tier, so nudges cannot break through Focus modes — schedule around the user's Focus schedule instead; notification content extensions and badges work fine.
- TimelineReloadPolicy options are atEnd (default), after(date), and never (app-driven via WidgetCenter); timeline entries should be spaced at least ~5 minutes apart; system reloads widgets for free on locale/Dynamic Type changes and handles StandBy display transitions automatically; budget learning takes a few days after install (widgets refresh generously at first, then settle).
- Priority ranking of surfaces for this specific app (personal, Ultra 3 owner, RPG layer): 1) Live Activity + Dynamic Island during workouts (biggest wow, works free, auto-appears on watch), 2) systemSmall/Medium Home Screen widgets for rings/streaks/XP with app-open-driven refresh, 3) StandBy as bedside recovery/sleep display, 4) watch complications only if a native watch app is built (costs 2 extra bundle IDs), 5) interactive widget buttons for one-tap logging (water/meal/mood), 6) local notification nudges, 7) App Intents for Action button + Shortcuts automation.
- The RPG/avatar layer maps naturally onto widget constraints: an XP/level systemSmall widget refreshed on app-open (no budget pressure since the character only changes when the user does something in-app), Live Activity showing the avatar 'earning XP' during a workout (4KB limit means avatar art must ship in the widget bundle's asset catalog, not in the dynamic payload — only send state enums/numbers and swap pre-bundled images), and streak-risk local notifications styled as quest reminders.
- Since the app can be foregrounded to refresh everything and APNs is unavailable, do not architect around server pushes at all: the update spine is (a) HealthKit background delivery wakes app hourly-ish, (b) app recomputes calories/recovery/XP, writes App Group snapshot, reloads widget timelines, (c) during workouts the workout session/location background runtime drives the Live Activity directly — this whole loop is free-provisioning-clean.

## Specifics
- Free-tier ('Apple Developer' column) capabilities per Apple's official table: App Groups, Background Modes, Data Protection, HealthKit, HomeKit, Inter-App Audio, Keychain Sharing, Maps, Wireless Accessory Configuration — everything else (incl. Push Notifications, Time Sensitive Notifications, iCloud CloudKit/documents/KVS, Siri, Wallet, WeatherKit, Communication Notifications, Associated Domains) requires paid ADP
- Free provisioning limits: 7-day provisioning profile expiry (weekly re-deploy from Xcode), ~3 apps installed simultaneously, 10 App IDs per 7 days per team; each target (app, widget extension, watch app, watch widget extension) consumes one App ID
- Widget refresh budget: 40-70 reloads/day per widget (~every 15-60 min), rolling 24h window; free reloads when app foregrounded, active audio/navigation session, App Intent interaction, widget animation, locale/Dynamic Type change
- WidgetCenter.shared.reloadTimelines(ofKind:) / reloadAllTimelines() / getCurrentConfigurations — call on app background/scenePhase change to refresh widgets outside budget
- TimelineReloadPolicy: .atEnd, .after(Date), .never; timeline entries >= ~5 min apart
- WidgetFamily cases: systemSmall/systemMedium/systemLarge/systemExtraLarge (iOS 14+ Home Screen), accessoryCircular/accessoryRectangular/accessoryInline (iOS 16+ Lock Screen AND watchOS 9+ complications/Smart Stack), accessoryCorner (watchOS 9+ only)
- AccessoryWidgetGroup (newer watchOS/iOS): label + three content views masked circle or rounded-square, styled via AccessoryWidgetGroupStyle
- Rendering modes: fullColor (Home Screen/StandBy), vibrant (Lock Screen, desaturated), accented (iOS 18 tinted Home Screen — system splits views into accent/primary groups via .widgetAccentable()); detect via @Environment(\.widgetRenderingMode)
- iOS 17+ widgets require containerBackground(for: .widget) {}; system removes this background in StandBy, Lock Screen, and accented mode — put the cream #F5F0E8-style background there so it strips cleanly, keep coral #D97757 only on accentable marks
- Lock Screen widget min font size: 11pt; StandBy = two systemSmall widgets side-by-side scaled up, Night Mode = monochrome red tint under low ambient light
- HealthKit entitlements: com.apple.developer.healthkit (+ com.apple.developer.healthkit.background-delivery for background delivery, iOS 15+); Info.plist NSHealthShareUsageDescription / NSHealthUpdateUsageDescription; HKHealthStore.isHealthDataAvailable() gate; one long-lived HKHealthStore per app
- HealthKit background delivery: enableBackgroundDelivery(for:frequency:withCompletion:) — stepCount capped at .hourly on iOS; most watchOS types hourly; immediate allowed for vo2Max, highHeartRateEvent, lowHeartRateEvent, irregularHeartRhythmEvent, numberOfTimesFallen, etc.; observer queries must be set up at app launch; 3 missed completion handlers stops delivery; not testable in Simulator
- Health data is NSFileProtection-style protected: unreadable while iPhone locked — widget extensions cannot reliably query HealthKit (and can never present authorization UI); use App Group (group.* identifier, free tier OK) snapshot pattern instead
- Live Activity payload limit: 4KB total static+dynamic ActivityAttributes/ContentState (applies to updates too); images must be pre-bundled in extension asset catalog and <= presentation resolution (minimal presentation ~45x36.67pt)
- Live Activity lifecycle: 8h max active, +4h Lock-Screen lingering (12h total); Activity.request() needs foreground (or LiveActivityIntent, iOS 17.2+); update(_:)/end() work from background; staleDate -> .stale state + isStale; relevanceScore decides which activity owns the Dynamic Island
- Live Activity Info.plist keys: NSSupportsLiveActivities = YES (required); NSSupportsLiveActivitiesFrequentUpdates = YES (only matters for push budget — irrelevant without APNs; local updates from background runtime are not budget-limited the same way)
- Live Activity presentations to implement: Lock Screen view, DynamicIsland { compactLeading, compactTrailing, minimal, expanded regions (.leading/.trailing/.center/.bottom) }; Always-On display: check @Environment(\.isLuminanceReduced), system disables animations
- iOS 18 + watchOS 11: iPhone Live Activities auto-appear in Apple Watch Smart Stack with zero watch code (system shows compactLeading+compactTrailing by default); ActivityConfiguration(...).supplementalActivityFamilies([.small]) + @Environment(\.activityFamily) customizes the watch small presentation
- HKWorkoutSession: watchOS 2.0+; iOS/iPadOS only since 17.0 (iPhone needs external BT heart-rate sensor for HR); one session per device (second start -> errorAnotherWorkoutSessionStarted); high-frequency HR samples + GPS route for outdoor activities; grants workout background runtime
- Workout mirroring: session.startMirroringToCompanionDevice(completion:) (watchOS 10/iOS 17+), stopMirroringToCompanionDevice, sendToRemoteWorkoutSession(data:) for bidirectional watch<->iPhone messaging — the sanctioned way for the iPhone app to get live watch metrics and drive a Live Activity
- Background Modes capability is free-tier: use 'location' mode for GPS route tracking (CoreLocation CLLocationManager.allowsBackgroundLocationUpdates) which simultaneously provides the background runtime to update the workout Live Activity locally every few seconds
- Interactive widgets (iOS 17+): only Button(intent:) and Toggle(isOn:intent:); AppIntent.perform() runs in widget extension process; openAppWhenRun=true / ForegroundContinuableIntent / LiveActivityIntent / AudioPlaybackIntent run in app process; buttons dead while device locked; await all writes before return .result() (system reloads timeline right after); .invalidatableContent() for pending values
- App Intents framework (iOS 16+, watchOS 9+): no entitlement; surfaces = Shortcuts, Spotlight, Siri (paid Siri capability NOT needed for Shortcuts phrase invocation), iPhone 15 Pro+/Watch Ultra Action button, Apple Pencil squeeze, Focus filters; AppShortcutsProvider makes intents appear in Shortcuts with zero user setup
- iOS 18 Controls: ControlWidget (ControlWidgetButton/ControlWidgetToggle) puts app actions in Control Center, Lock Screen bottom shortcuts, and Action button — free-tier compatible glanceable entry point for 'Start workout' / 'Log water'
- Local notifications (UNUserNotificationCenter): no entitlement; UNCalendarNotificationTrigger/UNTimeIntervalNotificationTrigger; UNNotificationAction buttons for one-tap logging from the nudge; .timeSensitive interruption level will NOT work on free tier (entitlement is paid) — use .active default; badges + custom sounds OK
- watchOS complication budget: app with complication on active watch face gets ~4 background refreshes/hour shared between HealthKit background delivery and WKApplicationRefreshBackgroundTask
- Watch complication targets: watch app + watch widget extension both needed for Ultra 3 face complications; accessoryCorner supports .widgetLabel gauge/text on Ultra's corner slots; same SwiftUI views reusable for iOS Lock Screen accessory widgets
- Provisioning-expiry failure mode: after day 7 the app refuses to launch, widgets show placeholder/blank, Live Activities can't start, complications go inert — plan a weekly 'plug in and hit Run' ritual or use Xcode over Wi-Fi; data in App Group persists across re-installs from same team
- Dynamic Island hardware: iPhone 14 Pro and later (all iPhone 15/16/17 non-SE models); older devices show Live Activity only as Lock Screen banner — Ultra 3 owner almost certainly has Dynamic Island hardware but implement Lock Screen presentation regardless (it is mandatory)
- Simulator caveats: HealthKit background delivery and provisioning-profile behavior only verifiable on physical devices; widget timelines debuggable in Simulator; test Live Activity budget/behavior on device

## Recommendations
- Build the workout Live Activity first: ActivityConfiguration with Lock Screen + full Dynamic Island regions, brand coral #D97757 progress ring / cream background (Live Activities keep custom colors), staleDate = now+60s during workouts, and supplementalActivityFamilies([.small]) so it auto-appears on the Watch Ultra 3 Smart Stack without writing a watch app initially.
- Adopt the App Group snapshot architecture from day one: main app owns all HealthKit queries, serializes a compact snapshot struct (rings, calories, XP, level, streak, recovery score) to the shared group container, and every widget/complication/Live Activity view reads only that snapshot; call WidgetCenter.shared.reloadAllTimelines() on every scenePhase -> background transition.
- Drive in-workout Live Activity updates with local background runtime, not push: use the 'location' background mode for iPhone GPS workouts, and HKWorkoutSession mirroring (startMirroringToCompanionDevice) if/when a watch app is added — never design anything that needs APNs, it is unavailable under free provisioning.
- Design every widget in three renderings: fullColor (cream/coral brand), vibrant (Lock Screen monochrome — rely on shape/gauge, 11pt+ text), and accented (iOS 18 tint — mark the XP ring and key stat with .widgetAccentable()); put the cream background only in containerBackground(for: .widget) so StandBy/tinted modes strip it cleanly.
- Ship exactly two widget sizes initially to conserve App IDs and scope: a systemSmall 'rings + avatar level' widget (doubles as the StandBy display) and an accessoryCircular/accessoryRectangular pair for Lock Screen; defer the native watch app + complications to phase 2 since iOS 18 already mirrors the Live Activity to the watch.
- Use App Intents everywhere for input: LogWaterIntent, LogMealIntent, StartWorkoutIntent exposed via AppShortcutsProvider — this gets Shortcuts, Spotlight, the Ultra 3 Action button, iOS 18 Control Center buttons, and interactive widget buttons all from one implementation, entirely free-tier.
- Keep nudges local and Focus-respecting: schedule UNCalendarNotificationTrigger reminders (streak-at-risk at 8pm, bedtime wind-down) with UNNotificationAction quick-log buttons; do not plan on time-sensitive breakthrough (paid entitlement) or server-driven pushes.
- Budget the RPG layer for widget constraints: pre-bundle all avatar sprite states in the widget extension's asset catalog and pass only enums/ints through timeline entries and the 4KB Live Activity payload; level-ups happen on app open (free reload window), so the widget avatar updates feel instant without spending refresh budget.
