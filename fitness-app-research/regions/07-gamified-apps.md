# gamified-apps

## Key findings
- Gentler Streak (Apple Watch App of the Year 2022) is the closest aesthetic/philosophy match to our goal: its 'Activity Path' is an adaptive daily training zone computed from ~10 days of workout load (intensity, duration, frequency) plus recovery signals, and critically its streak is forgiving — rest days and recovery days never break the streak; users can also set statuses (Active, On a Break, Sick, Injured) that reshape the path. This 'sustainable streak' is repeatedly cited in reviews as why people stay for years instead of churning after a broken chain.
- Habitica has the most reusable RPG math: XP to next level = 0.25*L^2 + 10*L + 139.75 (rounded to nearest 10), four stats (STR/CON/PER/INT), +1 allocatable stat point per level up to a hard cap at level 100, and a damage mechanic where missed dailies hurt HP while CON reduces that damage. Its weakness for fitness is that XP comes from self-reported task checkoffs, not measured activity.
- WalkScape (RuneScape-like walking MMO) drives ALL progression from pedometer steps: 10 skills each capped at level 99 on the OSRS exponential curve (xpEquate(l) = floor(l + 300*2^(l/7)), summed, /4, WalkScape scales by ~4.6 for character level), plus a 'Step Bank' that banks steps taken while idle and doubles the next N real steps — a clever forgiveness/rollover mechanic. Its exponential curve works only because steps are effectively unbounded; for workout-based XP a quadratic (Habitica-style) curve paces better.
- Fitness RPG (Shikudo) converts steps into 'energy' used to level a team of heroes; reviews respect the step-to-power core loop but consistently cite its failure modes: 9+ confusing currencies, grind after the storyline ends, and pay-to-progress pressure. Lesson: one XP currency, no economy sprawl.
- Zombies, Run! proves narrative + interval mechanics work: zombie chases require running 20% faster than your rolling 30-second average pace for ~100 seconds (interval training disguised as gameplay), and collected supplies feed a base-building metagame between runs. Retention comes from story ownership, not points.
- Pokemon GO's Adventure Sync is the canonical background-fitness design: HealthKit distance counts while the app is closed, with distance-gated rewards (2/5/10 km eggs; buddy candy at 1/3/5/20 km per candy), weekly milestone rewards (e.g., 50 km = 10,000 stardust), push notifications on near-completion ('egg about to hatch'), and an anti-cheat speed cap (~10.5 km/h) that discards faster-than-walking distance.
- Ring Fit Adventure paces unlocks over months: Attack/Defense stats rise with level, the skill tree only unlocks at level 40 (expanding at 65/130, maxing at 285), and skill points are allocated across exercise-type branches (arms, legs, core, yoga). Designers deliberately drip-feed systems so there is always a next unlock after weeks of exercise.
- Finch's retention loop is emotional, not competitive: tasks give the pet 'energy', energy sends the pet on a 6-hour adventure, and the user returns later to hear about it — a time-shifted second session hook. A home-screen widget showing the pet plus a progress bar is credited (Deconstructor of Fun teardown) as a major retention driver. The pet frames self-care as caring for something, which removes shame on bad days.
- Duolingo streak data quantifies forgiveness: Streak Freeze reduced churn 21% among at-risk users, streak wagers improved D7 retention ~14%, and loss aversion kicks in around day 7 of a streak. Design rule: a streak that cannot be repaired is a time bomb for a bad day; every durable streak system ships freezes/repair.
- A 2024 Lancet eClinicalMedicine meta-analysis of RCTs found gamified health apps improve physical activity with small-to-medium effect (Hedges g = 0.42) vs non-gamified, with effects persisting post-intervention — gamification is not just a novelty spike when designed around progress feedback.
- Motivation research warns of the overjustification trap: tangible/controlling rewards undermine intrinsic motivation (meta-analytic effect ~ -0.36), while informational feedback that signals competence and progress enhances it. XP/levels should be framed as a progress mirror of real fitness, not a payment for exercising.
- Self-determination theory maps cleanly onto the design: autonomy (choose your own workouts, no forced dailies), competence (visible stat growth, adaptive difficulty), relatedness (less relevant for a personal app; the avatar/pet substitutes as an emotional bond). A 2025 Frontiers study found an S-shaped relationship between gamification feature richness and adherence — a moderate, coherent set of mechanics beats many bolted-on ones.
- Standard fitness apps lose ~73% of users in month one and intrinsic exercise motivation takes ~6-8 weeks to develop; the gamification layer's real job is bridging those first 6-8 weeks until the habit self-sustains.
- Solo Leveling-inspired apps (Arise: 'Level Up In Real Life', LEVELING: Fitness, ARISE SOLO) validate demand for exactly our concept — anime avatar, E-to-S rank progression, STR/AGI/STA stats rising from logged workouts, daily quest XP ('Seeing my stats increase is the ultimate dopamine hit') — but are ruined by 8-10 ads per session and self-reported workouts. A personal HealthKit-verified version has no such integrity or monetization problems, and 'earned not logged' XP (sensor-verified) is a differentiator none of them have.
- Streak mechanics consensus across apps: count a day as 'kept' if EITHER a workout happened OR a legitimate recovery condition holds (Gentler Streak's model), auto-grant 1 freeze per week of activity (Duolingo's model), and never allow more than loss of the counter — never claw back XP/levels already earned (loss should apply to the streak only, not progression).

## Specifics
- Habitica level curve: XP_to_next(L) = round_to_10(0.25*L^2 + 10*L + 139.75); stats STR/CON/PER/INT; +1 free stat point per level, all frozen at level 100; task XP multipliers trivial 0.6 / easy 6 / medium 9 / hard 12; INT increases XP earned; missed dailies deal HP damage reduced by CON.
- OSRS/WalkScape curve: total XP for level L = sum_{l=1}^{L-1} floor((l + 300*2^(l/7))/4); level 99 cap; WalkScape character level scales this by ~4.6 and is driven purely by lifetime steps; 10 skills (Agility, Carpentry, Cooking, Crafting, Foraging, Fishing, Mining, Smithing, Trinketry, Woodcutting) each trained by steps allocated to a chosen action.
- WalkScape 'Step Bank': steps taken while no action is active are banked; banked steps double the value of subsequent real steps — adaptable as an XP rollover/rest-day bonus.
- Pokemon GO distance gates: eggs 2/5/7/10/12 km; buddy candy per 1/3/5/20 km; 'Excited' buddy state = +1 bonus candy per km; weekly Adventure Sync milestones (e.g., 50 km -> 10,000 stardust); anti-cheat discards segments faster than ~10.5 km/h.
- Ring Fit Adventure: skill tree gated at level 40, expansions at 65 and 130, max level 285; skill points spent in per-muscle-group branches (arms/legs/core/yoga); stats limited to Attack and Defense.
- Zombies, Run! chase formula: maintain pace >= 120% of your trailing 30-second average for ~100 seconds or drop collected supplies; supplies spent on base-building between runs.
- Duolingo numbers: Streak Freeze -21% churn for at-risk users; streak wager +14% D7 retention; D1 retention grew 12% -> 55% over years of streak-system iteration; loss aversion effect emerges ~day 7.
- Research numbers: gamified vs non-gamified PA effect Hedges g = 0.42 (2024 Lancet eClinicalMedicine meta-analysis of RCTs, effects persist at follow-up); tangible-reward undermining of intrinsic motivation ~= -0.36; top behavior-change techniques = goal setting, action planning, self-monitoring; ~73% month-1 churn for standard fitness apps; intrinsic motivation forms over ~6-8 weeks; S-shaped returns to gamification feature count (moderate is optimal).
- Gentler Streak mechanics: Activity Path = adaptive daily min/max training zone from ~10 days of load (intensity, duration, frequency) + recovery; daily readiness/condition indicator; rest and 'active recovery' days count toward the streak; user statuses Active / On a Break / Sick / Injured modify recommendations; post-workout RPE logging on 0-10 scale; 100+ workout types auto-imported from Apple Health.
- Finch loop: tasks -> pet energy -> 6-hour adventure -> return-later payoff (second daily session); rewards = energy, outfits, room decor; widget with pet + daily progress bar is a primary retention surface.
- PROPOSED XP MODEL (synthesized): XP_day = sum over workouts of [minutes_in_zone * zone_mult] + floor(steps/1000) + floor(activeEnergy_kcal/50), with zone_mult = Z1:1, Z2:1.5, Z3:2, Z4:3, Z5:4 (zones from user's max HR via HealthKit heartRate samples during HKWorkout). Soft daily cap: XP beyond 300/day earns at 25% rate (rewards consistency over binging, mirrors research).
- PROPOSED LEVEL CURVE: Habitica-style quadratic XP_to_next(L) = round(0.25*L^2 + 10*L + 140). At ~150-250 XP/day (one real workout + normal movement), this yields a level every 1-2 days at L1-10, every ~3-4 days at L25, ~1 week at L50 — sustained but slowing, no hard cap needed before L100.
- PROPOSED STAT MAPPING: STR <- strength/functional workouts (HKWorkoutActivityType .traditionalStrengthTraining, .functionalStrengthTraining, .coreTraining): XP from minutes + optional tonnage; STA <- steady cardio (.running, .cycling, .swimming, .walking, .hiking) weighted by Z2-Z3 minutes, with VO2max (HKQuantityTypeIdentifier.vo2Max) uptrend granting bonus XP; AGI <- .highIntensityIntervalTraining, sports types, .flexibility/.yoga and Z4-Z5 interval minutes; VIT (recovery stat, Habitica-CON analog) <- sleep >= goal (HKCategoryTypeIdentifier.sleepAnalysis), HRV trend (heartRateVariabilitySDNN), restingHeartRate downtrend, and honoring recommended rest days. Each stat has its own XP pool on the same quadratic curve at ~50% rate; character level = f(total XP across stats).
- PROPOSED STREAK: 'momentum' day = (any workout >= 15 exercise minutes) OR (step goal met) OR (recovery day when readiness is low / user status is Sick/Injured — Gentler model); auto-earn 1 streak freeze per 7 kept days (max 2 banked, Duolingo model); streak affects nothing but itself and cosmetic flair — never gates XP.
- PROPOSED AVATAR PROGRESSION: anime avatar visual evolution at level milestones 5/10/20/35/50/75 (new outfit/aura tiers, Ring Fit-style drip-feed); rank letters E->D->C->B->A->S mapped to level bands (Arise model, e.g., E:1-9, D:10-19, C:20-34, B:35-49, A:50-74, S:75+); stat radar chart (STR/STA/AGI/VIT) as the avatar's 'status window'.
- HealthKit identifiers needed for the XP engine: activeEnergyBurned, appleExerciseTime, stepCount, distanceWalkingRunning, heartRate, restingHeartRate, heartRateVariabilitySDNN, vo2Max, sleepAnalysis, HKWorkout + workoutActivityType, appleStandHour; Apple Watch Ultra 3 supplies all of these via HKObserverQuery/HKAnchoredObjectQuery background delivery.
- Anti-patterns catalogued from reviews: multiple currencies (Fitness RPG's 9+), ad interruptions (Arise's 8-10/session), unforgiving streaks (Apple Rings' all-or-nothing), XP for self-reported unverified workouts (all Solo Leveling clones), pay-gated progression, and leaderboard-first design (undermines intrinsic motivation for solo users).
- Failure-tolerant design specifics: never deduct earned XP/levels; damage-style penalties (Habitica HP) are optional and should target a renewable resource (e.g., avatar 'energy'), not progression; streak repair must exist; low-readiness days should convert to 'rest quests' (sleep 8h, walk 10 min) that keep the loop alive (Finch/Gentler hybrid).
- Visual style notes: Gentler Streak = soft gradients, hand-illustrated feel, generous whitespace, single hero chart — closest existing app to a Claude-aesthetic fitness app; Finch = flat pastel illustration with rounded shapes; Arise/LEVELING = dark neon 'system window' UI (opposite of our cream/coral direction, but its 'status window' framing of stats is worth stealing in warm-palette form).

## Recommendations
- Adopt the hybrid model: Habitica's quadratic level curve + WalkScape's sensor-verified XP sourcing + Gentler Streak's forgiving streak + Arise's stat/rank framing (STR/STA/AGI/VIT, ranks E->S) + Finch-style avatar bond. Keep exactly one currency (XP); no coins, gems, or economy.
- Compute XP only from HealthKit-verified data (HR-zone minutes, steps, active energy) so levels are 'earned, not logged' — this preserves the informational (competence-signaling) framing that research shows protects intrinsic motivation, and it is the one thing App Store Solo Leveling clones cannot do honestly.
- Make the streak forgiveness-first: rest/recovery days count when readiness is low or a status (Sick/Injured/Travel) is set, auto-bank one freeze per kept week, and never let streak loss touch XP or levels. Loss aversion should protect the habit, not punish the person.
- Apply a soft daily XP cap (~300 XP, then 25% earn rate) so leveling rewards consistency over weekend binges — this aligns the game layer with actual training-load best practice and mirrors Gentler Streak's anti-overtraining stance.
- Drip-feed content Ring Fit-style: avatar evolutions and cosmetic unlocks at level milestones (5/10/20/35/50/75) rather than everything at once; the S-shaped feature-richness finding says ship 4-5 coherent mechanics (XP, levels, stats, streak, avatar) and stop.
- Steal Finch's time-shifted hook and widget: a WidgetKit widget showing the avatar + today's XP progress bar, and an evening 'day recap' moment (XP earned, stat gains, streak status) as the second daily touchpoint — no push-notification nagging.
- Map stats to workout families via HKWorkoutActivityType so the RPG layer nudges balanced training: heavy STR with lagging STA becomes visible on the avatar's radar chart, gently steering variety without prescribing workouts (autonomy support per SDT).
- Include a VIT/recovery stat fed by sleep, HRV, and resting HR so recovery is progression, not absence of progression — the single most consistent lesson from Gentler Streak's success and Ring/streak-app burnout complaints.
