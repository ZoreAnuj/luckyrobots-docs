# running-apps

## Key findings
- Nike Run Club's core loop is audio-guided runs (~300 in library, coached by Nike coaches/athletes/Headspace) plus a simple cumulative-distance leveling system (7 colored levels from Yellow to Volt) — the most successful lightweight gamification in running apps and a direct template for an RPG layer: level = f(lifetime km), with level color themed throughout the UI including on the Watch during workouts.
- Runna is the current gold standard for adaptive training plans: race-time prediction from user-stated ability + population data, pace targets derived from Estimated Race Time, and a 'Pace Insights' feature that reviews completed runs and auto-adjusts targets if you're consistently off pace; plans mix intervals (at/near lactate threshold with rest recoveries), tempos (continuous comfortably-hard blocks), long runs, time trials (confidence/fitness checks), plus optional strength & mobility sessions. Strava acquired Runna in 2025, confirming this is the category-winning model.
- Runna's Apple Watch app is the best reference implementation for your Watch companion: live pace targets with speed-up/slow-down alerts (audio + haptic vibration), on-screen step-by-step interval guidance, per-km/mi split readouts, Workout Mirroring between Watch and iPhone (watchOS mirroring API), and automatic post-run save to HealthKit so the run appears in Apple Fitness and syncs back with all watch-captured data.
- Runkeeper (ASICS) demonstrates the cleanest post-run analysis pattern for a minimal app: split times table, interactive route map, and pace analysis where you scroll back through splits to see exactly where you sped up or slowed down; its audio coach calls out splits, prompts pace corrections vs target, and gives milestone encouragement; training plans are calendar-slotted around user-chosen rest days and auto-log completed sessions against the plan.
- TrainingPeaks' TSS/CTL/ATL/TSB model is the canonical training-load math worth cloning: TSS = duration x intensity composite (1hr at threshold = 100 pts); CTL 'Fitness' = 42-day exponentially-weighted average of daily TSS; ATL 'Fatigue' = 7-day average; TSB 'Form' = yesterday's CTL minus yesterday's ATL (positive = race-ready, negative = normal during build blocks). Its structured workout builder uses drag-and-drop blocks with distance/duration + intensity targets by pace/HR/power/RPE — but its mobile app is widely criticized for not letting you build workouts on the phone, a gap a personal app avoids.
- Garmin Connect is the analysis benchmark: activity pages show full chart stack (pace, HR, elevation, power, stamina, performance condition), map, and lap breakdown, and recently added the ability to switch chart X-axis between time and distance (distance-axis makes hills/splits line up with the map). Its Race Predictor projects 5K-marathon times primarily from Firstbeat VO2max estimate (HR + pace during outdoor GPS runs, within ~5% of lab with chest strap) plus training history; Training Readiness combines sleep quality, recovery time, HRV status, and acute load; Daily Suggested Workouts prescribe the session type filling the biggest gap (aerobic/anaerobic/recovery).
- HealthFit proves the exact architecture your app should use: a local-only iOS app that mines Apple Health/Watch data (no cloud, no account), computes CTL/ATL/TSB from HealthKit workouts, offers customizable HR zones, tracks HRV/RHR/VO2max/sleep/weight, renders training-load charts, heatmaps, and per-workout charts, and exports FIT/GPX/CSV — all from HealthKit data alone, confirming everything needed is available on-device.
- Footpath's signature interactions for route planning: finger-trace drawing that snaps to roads/trails (also rivers/canals), instant distance + elevation measurement, pinch-to-zoom elevation profile with color-coded slope analysis and per-segment distance/gain/loss stats, and turn-by-turn voice navigation of custom routes on iPhone and Apple Watch.
- Strava's activity page (the pattern to copy for post-run screens): splits shown as a horizontal bar graph of pace per lap with numeric table below (distance, elapsed time, pace, plus HR/elevation per split); pace and HR charts are overlaid on a shaded elevation profile so terrain context is always visible; scrubbing/dragging on any chart highlights a section and recomputes stats for just that selection, with the corresponding map segment highlighted.
- Race prediction math used across apps: Riegel formula T2 = T1 x (D2/D1)^1.06 (~80% accurate, best for newer runners) and Jack Daniels' VDOT (pseudo-VO2max combining aerobic capacity + running economy; better for well-trained runners), which yields five training pace zones — Easy, Marathon, Threshold, Interval (95-100% VO2max), Repetition — read from the VDOT tables; typical app pipeline is race result -> Riegel to normalize distance -> VDOT -> zone paces.
- Apple's WorkoutKit (iOS 17+/watchOS 10+) lets a phone app compose custom interval workouts (warmup, repeatable work/recovery blocks, cooldown, each with distance/time goals and pace/cadence/power/HR alerts — current or average, range or threshold) and send them to the native Watch Workout app with a built-in preview UI; watchOS 11 added indoor-running pace alerts. This means structured Runna-style sessions can be delivered to the Ultra 3 without writing a full watch workout engine.
- For GPS routes, HKWorkoutRouteBuilder attaches CLLocation streams to HKWorkouts (insertRouteData + finishRoute(with:metadata:)); routes are read back as HKWorkoutRoute and rendered as MapKit polylines in SwiftUI Map; HKWorkout also exposes Fitness-app-style split data; a known edge case is very long routes (5+ hr, ~6000 samples) needing chunked insertion.
- Nike Run Club's gamification stack beyond levels: milestone badges at race distances (5K/10K/half/marathon), cumulative lifetime-distance badges, weekly streaks, PB tracking across distances/paces, plan-completion rewards, and time-limited challenge badges — PBs (fastest 1K/mile/5K/10K, longest run, most elevation) are surfaced prominently on the post-run summary itself, which is the highest-leverage moment for reward feedback.

## Specifics
- NRC Run Levels (lifetime distance): Yellow 0-49km, Orange 50-249km, Green 250-999km, Blue 1000-2499km, Purple 2500-4999km, Black 5000-14999km, Volt 15000+km; level color skins the whole app and Watch UI
- NRC post-run summary fields: distance, avg pace, total time, elevation gain, cadence, avg HR, mile/km splits, PB callouts
- Riegel: T2 = T1 x (D2/D1)^1.06 (performance degrades ~6% per doubling of distance)
- Daniels VDOT five zones: Easy, Marathon, Threshold (lactate threshold), Interval (95-100% VO2max), Repetition — derived from any race result via Daniels-Gilbert formula
- TrainingPeaks: TSS (1hr at FTP/threshold = 100), CTL = 42-day EWMA of TSS ('Fitness'), ATL = 7-day EWMA ('Fatigue'), TSB = CTL_yesterday - ATL_yesterday ('Form'); rTSS for running uses pace vs threshold pace
- HealthFit computes CTL/ALT/TSB, heatmaps, Explorer Score, customizable HR zones, HRV/RHR/VO2max(Cardio Fitness)/sleep/weight/BMI/body-fat tracking, entirely from local HealthKit data with no cloud; exports FIT/GPX/CSV/Markdown reports with charts
- Runna workout taxonomy: Intervals (e.g. 6x400m at 5K pace, walk/static recovery), Tempo (continuous comfortably-hard below threshold), Long runs (some with pace targets), Time Trials, Easy runs, plus strength & mobility sessions; Plan Difficulty knob controls count/intensity of hard sessions per week; Training Focus knob: speed vs endurance vs balanced
- Runna race prediction inputs: stated running ability + training schedule + population data from similar runners; Estimated Race Time drives all pace targets; 'Pace Insights' auto-adapts targets when consistently off pace
- Runna Watch features: pace range alerts (audio+haptic speed-up/slow-down), per-km split readouts, on-screen interval step guidance, Workout Mirroring (Watch<->iPhone live data), auto-save to Apple Health/Fitness
- Runkeeper training plan categories: 'Running for exercise' vs 'Train for a race' (5K, 10K, half, marathon, base building); plans slot workouts into calendar around chosen rest days and auto-mark sessions complete
- Runkeeper audio coach behaviors: split callouts, target-pace speed-up/ease-off prompts, milestone encouragement; post-run: splits table + interactive route map + pace analysis scrollable per-split
- Garmin activity chart stack: pace, HR, elevation, power, stamina, performance condition; X-axis toggleable between time and distance; laps table + map on same page
- Garmin Race Predictor = f(Firstbeat VO2max estimate from HR+pace on outdoor GPS runs, training load/history); VO2max within ~5% of lab with chest strap; Training Readiness = f(sleep quality, recovery time, HRV status, acute load); Daily Suggested Workout picks type (base/tempo/threshold/VO2max/anaerobic/recovery) to fill current training gap
- Strava splits UI: bar chart of pace per lap + table (distance, time, pace, HR, elev per split); pace/HR line charts overlaid on shaded elevation area; drag-to-select chart region recomputes stats and highlights map segment; Run Pace Zone Analysis shows time-in-zone per run
- Footpath: finger-trace route drawing with snap-to-roads/trails, elevation profile with pinch-zoom + color-coded slope + per-climb gain/loss stats, turn-by-turn voice nav on iPhone/Watch, offline maps (Elite tier)
- WorkoutKit (iOS 17+): CustomWorkout with warmup/cooldown steps and IntervalBlock(repeats:) of work/recovery IntervalSteps; each step takes a goal (distance/time/energy/open) and WorkoutAlert — pace (current or average), cadence, power (current/average, watchOS 10.4+), heart rate; range or threshold alert types; workoutPlan presented via built-in preview UI then appears in native Watch Workout app; watchOS 11 adds indoor-run pace alerts
- HealthKit route APIs: HKWorkoutRouteBuilder.insertRouteData([CLLocation]) during session, finishRoute(with:workout,metadata:) after; read via HKWorkoutRouteQuery streaming CLLocations; render as MKPolyline / SwiftUI MapPolyline; chunk insertion for 5000+ location samples
- HKLiveWorkoutBuilder + HKWorkoutSession on watchOS = live metrics (HR, distance, pace, cadence, active energy) and automatic ring credit; HKWorkout exposes native splits (HKWorkoutActivity/events) like the Fitness app
- HealthKit identifiers relevant to running analysis: HKQuantityTypeIdentifier.runningSpeed, runningPower, runningStrideLength, runningGroundContactTime, runningVerticalOscillation (all captured natively by Apple Watch), heartRate, heartRateVariabilitySDNN, restingHeartRate, vo2Max, distanceWalkingRunning, activeEnergyBurned, stepCount (cadence via HKLiveWorkoutBuilder)
- NRC library: ~300 audio-guided runs (recovery, speed/interval, long, 5K/10K, treadmill, Headspace mindful runs); 6 training plans incl. 4-week beginner 'Get Started' (~3 runs/week, 5-42min)
- TrainingPeaks structured workout model: blocks defined by duration-or-distance x intensity target (%FTP, %threshold pace, HR zone, RPE), drag-and-drop assembly, pushed to devices for real-time prompts; post-workout compliance coloring (green/yellow/red vs planned)
- Apps' HealthKit posture: NRC/Runkeeper write workouts+routes to HealthKit and sync wearables; Runna reads watch workouts back; HealthFit is read-only consumer of HealthKit; Garmin Connect writes via its own sync
- Common race-predictor caveat (the5krunner): predictions are benchmarks of aerobic capacity not race readiness — marathon estimates skew optimistic without high weekly mileage; useful to label predictions as 'fitness equivalent' not promises

## Recommendations
- Copy HealthFit's architecture wholesale: local-only, read/write HealthKit, no accounts or cloud — compute CTL/ATL/TSB (42/7-day EWMA of a run-TSS you derive from duration x intensity vs threshold pace or HR) for a Fitness/Fatigue/Form card; this is cheap to implement and is the single most 'pro' feature you can add.
- Build the post-run screen as the Strava/Garmin hybrid: hero map with route polyline, stat grid (distance, time, avg pace, avg HR, elevation gain, cadence, calories), splits section as horizontal pace bars + table with per-split HR/elevation, then pace and HR line charts overlaid on a shaded elevation area (coral line on cream, elevation in a muted warm gray); support drag-to-scrub with a synced map cursor, and offer distance as the chart X-axis.
- Use HKWorkoutSession + HKLiveWorkoutBuilder + HKWorkoutRouteBuilder on the Watch for recording, and read Apple's native running-form metrics (runningPower, strideLength, groundContactTime, verticalOscillation) the Ultra 3 already captures — free advanced metrics no third-party app surfaces well.
- Model the RPG layer on NRC's proven system: level = lifetime distance with named tiers and a tier color/aura applied to the avatar and accents (map onto anime-avatar evolution stages), plus milestone badges (first 5K/10K/half), streaks, and PB detection surfaced immediately on the post-run summary — that moment is where gamification reward lands hardest.
- Implement race predictions and pace zones with Riegel (T2 = T1 x (D2/D1)^1.06) + Daniels VDOT tables from the user's best recent efforts (mineable from HealthKit history); derive Easy/Marathon/Threshold/Interval/Repetition paces and use them to color-code every run and split by zone.
- Use WorkoutKit to compose interval sessions (warmup + repeat work/recovery blocks + cooldown with pace-range alerts) and push them to the native Watch Workout app — you get Apple's interval engine, haptics, and pace alerts for free instead of building a custom watch workout UI; this covers 90% of Runna's watch value.
- Keep training plans Runkeeper-simple, not TrainingPeaks-complex: a weekly calendar of typed sessions (easy/tempo/interval/long/rest) auto-checked-off when a matching HealthKit workout appears, with one Runna-style adaptive rule — nudge pace targets when the user consistently beats or misses them.
- Adopt Footpath-style route planning only if scope allows: snap-to-road finger tracing via MapKit directions is a large lift; the higher-value/lower-cost piece is its elevation-profile pattern (color-coded slope, per-segment gain/loss) applied to completed runs.
