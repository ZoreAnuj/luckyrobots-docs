# apple-native

## Key findings
- NOTE ON METHOD: live web fetch/search was blocked by the session gateway (403 policy denials on all external hosts), so this teardown is from model knowledge current through iOS 26 / watchOS 26 (released Sept 2025) and HealthKit SDKs through iOS 18/26; no URLs were actually fetched. Facts marked 'approx' should be spot-checked in Xcode docs during implementation.
- Apple's workout RECORDING stack is essentially unbeatable and fully readable by third parties: the watchOS Workout app records 50+ workout types with dual-band GPS (Ultra), HR, running dynamics (power, cadence, stride length, ground contact time, vertical oscillation), cycling power/FTP, elevation, splits, and auto-transitioning triathlon/multisport mode — and every workout, including its full GPS route, is readable via HealthKit (HKWorkout + HKWorkoutRoute via HKWorkoutRouteQuery). Do NOT rebuild a watch workout tracker.
- watchOS 26 Workout app added: a redesigned four-corner-button layout, Workout Buddy (Apple Intelligence generated-voice encouragement using your fitness history — rings, PRs, milestones; limited workout types, English only, requires Apple Intelligence iPhone), Apple Music media auto-suggestions per workout type, and creation of custom interval workouts from the iPhone Fitness app.
- Apple's structured-workout features (Custom Workouts with warmup/work/recovery/cooldown intervals, Pacer with ahead/behind alerts, Race Route to race your past self on frequently-run GPS routes, pace/HR-zone/cadence/power alerts) are strong AND third-party apps can inject structured workouts into Apple's own Workout app via WorkoutKit (iOS 17+) — so a personal app can author intervals on iPhone and have Apple's watch UI execute them.
- Activity Rings (Move/Exercise/Stand) are Apple's core engagement loop and are fully exposed via HKActivitySummaryQuery, including goals and progress; watchOS 11+ added per-weekday goal schedules and ring pausing/rest days that preserve award streaks. Re-render rings in your own aesthetic if you like, but do not reinvent the goal model.
- Training Load (watchOS 11+) compares 7-day exertion vs 28-day baseline into classifications (well below / below / steady / above / well above) driven by a 1-10 effort rating (auto-estimated for cardio, manually rated for strength). The underlying per-workout effort IS exposed to third parties (workoutEffortScore / estimatedWorkoutEffortScore, HKUnit appleEffortScore, iOS 18+), but Apple's 7v28 classification itself is NOT — you can cheaply recompute it yourself from the raw scores.
- The Vitals app (watchOS 11+) does overnight baseline/outlier detection on heart rate, HRV/respiratory rate, wrist temperature, blood oxygen, and sleep duration, flagging nights when 2+ metrics are atypical — but it is binary outlier detection, NOT a 0-100 readiness/recovery score like Whoop or Garmin Body Battery. All the underlying overnight metrics are readable via HealthKit, so a custom recovery score is buildable and is a genuine gap.
- watchOS 26 / iOS 26 added a Sleep Score (0-100, from duration + bedtime consistency + interruptions, approx weighting 50/30/20 with bands Very Low→Very High) and FDA-cleared hypertension notifications (30-day optical-sensor pattern analysis, Series 9+/Ultra 2+). Sleep stages, duration, and wrist temp are readable via HealthKit; the sleep SCORE itself and hypertension events are NOT exposed to third parties.
- Apple's nutrition story is its biggest functional hole: the Health app defines ~35 dietary HealthKit types (energy, macros, ~30 micronutrients, water, caffeine) but ships NO food database, NO logging UI, and no calories-in-vs-out view. Every serious Apple user pairs a third-party logger. This is the clearest BUILD item: your own minimal logger writing dietaryEnergyConsumed/protein/carbs/fat to HealthKit.
- There is no unified daily view anywhere in Apple's stack: workouts and rings live in Fitness, sleep/HR/vitals in Health, recovery signals in the watch Vitals app, and nutrition nowhere. Highlights and Trends (90-day vs 365-day comparisons across ~6 metrics) are slow, retrospective, and non-correlational — Apple never answers 'how did last night's sleep affect today's run'. A single-screen daily dashboard with cross-metric correlation is the app's core value proposition.
- Apple's gamification is shallow: award badges (streaks, monthly challenges personalized to your history, limited-edition event awards), ring-close streaks, and 1v1 Activity Competitions (points = ring percentages, capped ~600/day). There is no XP, no levels, no avatar, no progression economy, and awards data has NO public API. An RPG/leveling layer fed by HealthKit metrics has zero overlap with anything Apple ships.
- Fitness app Trends coaching, Cardio Fitness (VO2max estimates with low-fitness notifications), Cardio Recovery (1-min heart rate recovery), heart-rate zones (auto-computed from HR reserve), and cycling FTP/power zones are all computed by Apple for free and exposed as HealthKit types (vo2Max, heartRateRecoveryOneMinute, cyclingFunctionalThresholdPower) — read them, never recompute.
- Third-party watch/phone apps get real parity primitives when needed later: HKLiveWorkoutBuilder sessions on watchOS contribute to Activity rings and get background sensor runtime; HKWorkoutSession mirroring (iOS 17+) live-streams a watch workout to the iPhone; HKAnchoredObjectQuery + enableBackgroundDelivery give incremental background sync of new samples.
- Free-provisioning sideloading constraint check: HealthKit works with a free Apple developer account (it is one of the few entitlements allowed), but profiles expire every 7 days requiring re-deploy from Xcode; avoid APNs-dependent features and prefer local notifications; iCloud/CloudKit entitlements are NOT available on free provisioning, so persist locally (HealthKit itself is the sync layer since it's on-device).
- Apple Fitness+ (subscription trainer-led content, custom plans) and Workout Buddy are content/AI plays with no API surface — irrelevant to build against, nothing to integrate.
- GPS route rendering is a pure read-and-draw problem: HKWorkoutRouteQuery streams CLLocation arrays for any recorded outdoor workout; MapKit (MKPolyline overlay, or MapKit for SwiftUI's MapPolyline) renders Strava-style route maps trivially, and you can style the map (standard/muted config) and polyline stroke in the coral #D97757 accent over a cream card. No location permission needed at runtime for historical routes — only HealthKit route authorization.

## Specifics
- HealthKit read types for the dashboard: activeEnergyBurned, basalEnergyBurned, stepCount, distanceWalkingRunning, appleExerciseTime, appleStandTime, flightsClimbed, heartRate, restingHeartRate, heartRateVariabilitySDNN, heartRateRecoveryOneMinute, vo2Max, respiratoryRate, oxygenSaturation, appleSleepingWristTemperature, timeInDaylight, physicalEffort, workoutEffortScore, estimatedWorkoutEffortScore
- Sleep: HKCategoryTypeIdentifier.sleepAnalysis with values asleepCore / asleepDeep / asleepREM / awake / inBed (stage tracking requires Watch, watchOS 9+); Apple's Sleep Score (watchOS 26) is NOT in HealthKit — recompute approx as duration+consistency+interruptions if wanted
- Nutrition write types: dietaryEnergyConsumed, dietaryProtein, dietaryCarbohydrates, dietaryFatTotal, dietaryFiber, dietarySugar, dietarySodium, dietaryWater, dietaryCaffeine plus ~25 more micronutrient identifiers (dietaryVitaminC, dietaryIron, etc.)
- Rings: HKActivitySummaryQuery returns HKActivitySummary with activeEnergyBurned/Goal, appleExerciseTime/Goal, appleStandHours/Goal per day — includes user's own goals; watchOS 11+ supports per-weekday goals and pausing rings without breaking streaks
- Workouts: HKWorkout (workoutActivityType ~80 enum cases, duration, totalEnergyBurned, totalDistance, workoutEvents for segments/laps/pauses); statistics(for:) for per-workout HR avg/max since iOS 16; HKWorkoutRoute samples fetched via HKWorkoutRouteQuery streaming [CLLocation] with timestamps, speed, altitude
- Running dynamics types (Watch-native since watchOS 9): runningPower (watts), runningSpeed, runningStrideLength, runningGroundContactTime, runningVerticalOscillation, cadence via runningStrideLength+speed or HKQuantityTypeIdentifier stepCount during workout
- Cycling types (iOS 17/watchOS 10): cyclingPower, cyclingCadence, cyclingSpeed, cyclingFunctionalThresholdPower; Watch pairs BT power/cadence/speed sensors natively; live workout shows as iPhone Live Activity 'bike computer' view
- WorkoutKit (iOS 17+): CustomWorkout (warmup/blocks of WorkoutStep intervals/cooldown), PacerWorkout (distance+time), SwimBikeRunWorkout; WorkoutPlan can be presented for preview and scheduled so it appears inside Apple's watch Workout app
- Effort scores: HKUnit.appleEffortScore(), 1-10 scale; write workoutEffortScore to rate strength sessions programmatically; relate to workout via HKWorkoutEffortRelationshipQuery (iOS 18)
- Training Load algorithm (Apple's, for replication): 7-day weighted load vs 28-day average, classified well below / below / steady / above / well above; effort auto-estimated for cardio workout types from HR, GPS, age/height/weight; strength/other prompt manual 1-10 rating at workout end
- Vitals app baseline: needs ~7 nights to establish typical range; tracks overnight HR, respiratory rate, wrist temperature, blood oxygen, sleep duration; flags 'outliers' when 2+ metrics deviate; classification not exposed via any API
- watchOS 26 Workout app: four-corner button layout (top-left Workout Buddy, top-right workout views, bottom-left media, bottom-right alerts/custom); Workout Buddy supported types: indoor/outdoor run, indoor/outdoor walk, outdoor cycle, HIIT, functional and traditional strength
- Race Route: available on frequently-repeated outdoor run/cycle routes, race against last or personal best effort with live ahead/behind delta; Pacer: target pace/finish-time with ahead/behind alerts — both watch-native, no API, but both replaceable-by-nothing (lean on)
- Triathlon/Multisport workout type: auto-detects swim-bike-run transitions via motion; logs each leg as separate segment within one HKWorkout with activityType .swimBikeRunTransition
- Heart Rate Zones: auto-calculated from heart rate reserve (customizable in settings), shown live in workout; zone time per workout not directly a HealthKit type — recompute from heartRate samples against user max/resting HR
- Awards/badges: monthly challenges are algorithmically personalized (e.g. 'burn 19,400 kcal this month'); limited-edition awards (Earth Day, Pride, Heart Month, National Fitness Day etc.); NO API access to award or streak data — streaks must be recomputed from HKActivitySummary history
- Activity Competitions: 7-day 1v1, daily points = sum of ring completion percentages capped at 600/day — a proven lightweight competitive mechanic worth borrowing for solo 'race your past week' gamification
- Ultra 3 hardware capabilities relevant to app: dual-band L1+L5 GPS (best-in-class route accuracy), depth gauge, 3000-nit display, up to 42h battery (72h low power), satellite SOS/messaging, action button (can trigger shortcuts/app intents), always-on altimeter
- Background sync pattern: HKObserverQuery + enableBackgroundDelivery(for:frequency:.immediate) to wake app on new samples; HKAnchoredObjectQuery with persisted HKQueryAnchor for incremental workout/sample fetch; statistics collections via HKStatisticsCollectionQuery for daily aggregates
- iOS 26 Health app structure: Summary (pinned favorites + Highlights auto-insights), Browse by category (Activity, Body Measurements, Cycle, Hearing, Heart, Medications, Mental Wellbeing incl. State of Mind + PHQ-9/GAD-7 screeners, Mobility, Nutrition, Respiratory, Sleep, Symptoms, Vitals); Trends = 90-day vs 365-day comparisons
- Hypertension notifications (watchOS 26, Series 9+/Ultra 2+, FDA-cleared Sept 2025): passive 30-day optical-sensor analysis for chronic hypertension patterns; events not exposed to HealthKit third parties
- Not exposed via any API (must rebuild or skip): Sleep Score, Training Load classification, Vitals outlier status, award/badge/streak objects, Fitness+ content, Workout Buddy, hypertension notification events
- Free provisioning constraints: HealthKit entitlement allowed on free account; 7-day profile expiry (re-run from Xcode weekly); no CloudKit, no APNs push (use UNUserNotificationCenter local notifications); background delivery entitlement (com.apple.developer.healthkit.background-delivery) works
- MapKit route rendering: MapPolyline (SwiftUI MapKit, iOS 17+) with .stroke(Color(hex:"D97757"), lineWidth:4) over Map with .mapStyle(.standard(pointsOfInterest:.excludingAll)) reproduces the Strava route-card look; MKMapSnapshotter for static route thumbnails in workout lists
- Calorie balance formula from HealthKit: TDEE = activeEnergyBurned + basalEnergyBurned (both continuously written by Watch); balance = dietaryEnergyConsumed - TDEE — Apple computes both sides but never shows them together anywhere in its UI
- Custom recovery score inputs all HealthKit-readable: overnight heartRateVariabilitySDNN vs 28-day baseline, restingHeartRate delta, appleSleepingWristTemperature deviation, respiratoryRate deviation, sleepAnalysis duration/stage mix, prior-day workoutEffortScore — z-score blend replicates Whoop-style readiness Apple lacks

## Recommendations
- LEAN ON APPLE (do not build): workout recording on the Watch (use Apple's Workout app — its GPS, sensors, intervals, pacer, race route, triathlon mode are best-in-class and fully readable afterward), sleep staging, VO2max/Cardio Recovery/FTP computation, ring goal model, and all sensor data collection. Your app should be a READ-heavy HealthKit client, not a tracker.
- BUILD the unified daily dashboard as the app's centerpiece: one screen combining rings (from HKActivitySummary), calories in vs out (dietaryEnergyConsumed vs active+basal energy — a view Apple computes both sides of but never shows), last night's sleep, recovery score, and today's workout with route thumbnail. This single screen out-executes Apple's fragmented Fitness/Health/Vitals split.
- BUILD nutrition logging (Apple's biggest hole): minimal manual/quick-add logger writing dietaryEnergyConsumed + macros to HealthKit so data stays in the ecosystem; skip building a food database — quick-add kcal/macros plus a few saved favorites fits the super-minimal brief.
- BUILD a custom 0-100 recovery/readiness score from HealthKit overnight metrics (HRV, RHR, wrist temp, respiratory rate, sleep) vs rolling 28-day baselines — Apple's Vitals app only does binary outlier flags, and its Sleep Score and Training Load classification have no API, but all raw inputs are readable and the 7-day-vs-28-day load math is trivially replicable from workoutEffortScore.
- BUILD the RPG/gamification layer entirely yourself — zero Apple overlap: XP from Move kcal, exercise minutes, workout effort scores, streaks, and closed rings; anime avatar leveling; borrow Apple's proven mechanics (daily point cap like Competitions' 600/day to prevent grinding, rest-day forgiveness like watchOS 11 ring pausing so the streak system doesn't punish recovery).
- For GPS route maps, read HKWorkoutRoute via HKWorkoutRouteQuery and render with SwiftUI MapKit MapPolyline stroked in the coral accent (#D97757) over a muted map style, with MKMapSnapshotter thumbnails in the workout feed — Strava-like visuals with no location tracking code of your own.
- If structured training is wanted later, use WorkoutKit to compose interval/pacer workouts on iPhone and schedule them INTO Apple's watch Workout app rather than writing any watchOS code — keeps the project iPhone-only initially, which matters given 7-day free-provisioning re-deploys.
- Design constraint from sideloading: no CloudKit and no push on free provisioning — persist app state locally (SwiftData/files), treat HealthKit as the source of truth and sync layer, use local notifications only, and keep the app resilient to weekly Xcode reinstalls (never store canonical data only in app sandbox; derive everything recomputable from HealthKit).
