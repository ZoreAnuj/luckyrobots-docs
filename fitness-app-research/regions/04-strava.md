# strava

## Key findings
- Strava's core engagement loop is: record activity -> auto-match to segments/past routes -> instant achievements (PRs, crowns, top-10s, badges) -> social feed with kudos/comments. Research shows kudos measurably increase running frequency, and the app's stickiness comes from this record-compare-share loop far more than from analytics.
- Pricing 2026: free tier vs one subscription tier at $11.99/mo or $79.99/yr. Free keeps unlimited GPS recording/uploads, feed, kudos, comments, clubs, challenges (individual), Beacon (phone-recorded only), segment matching with top-10-only leaderboards, basic activity stats, and dark mode. Paid: full/filtered leaderboards, Live Segments, route builder + offline maps, personal/night/weekly heatmaps, Fitness & Freshness, Relative Effort details, Best Efforts trends, Matched Runs, 3D Flyover, Group Challenges, Athlete Intelligence AI, workout analysis, goals, training log, and (new in 2025) Year in Sport recap.
- The dominant user complaint is 'paywall creep': features that were free being moved behind subscription one at a time (segment leaderboards in 2020, Group Challenges in Aug 2024, Year in Sport in Dec 2025), plus the Nov 2024 API crackdown that killed many third-party apps (and from June 2026, API access itself requires a paid subscription). Users describe eroding trust and value exchange.
- Fitness & Freshness is a Banister impulse-response model: daily Training Load (power-based) and/or Relative Effort (HR-based TRIMP) feed exponentially-decaying Fitness (CTL-like, ~42-day) and Fatigue (ATL-like, ~7-day) curves, with Form = Fitness - Fatigue. Relative Effort is a TRIMP variant that weights time-in-HR-zone with progressively higher coefficients per zone (Z5 >> Z2), and accepts manual Perceived Exertion when no HR data exists. Exact coefficients are proprietary.
- Best Efforts auto-detects fastest elapsed-time (not moving-time) splits at benchmark distances inside any run (400m to 50K for running; 5mi-180K plus power-duration curves and Biggest Climb/Elevation Gain for cycling), tracking top-3 lifetime and top-10 annual per distance — this is the single most-copied Strava analytics feature and requires only GPS+timestamps to compute.
- Map visualization stack: encoded polyline on activity page; 'Map Types'/#statmaps color the polyline by pace/speed (darker blue = faster), heart rate (darker red = higher), elevation (yellow low to black high), gradient (yellow descents, red climbs), power (darker purple), plus time, surface, temperature and seasonal novelty styles; 3D terrain Flyover (animated camera along the route, powered by acquired FATMAP); Personal Heatmap (private, all-time everywhere you've been), Global Heatmap, and subscriber-only Night (sunset-to-sunrise) and Weekly heatmaps.
- Apple Watch/HealthKit behavior: Strava has a native watchOS app (redesigned Sept 2025 with Live Segments; route navigation in beta Jan 2026) but reviewers call it basic vs Apple Workout/WorkOutDoors (few stats, little customization, laggy maps, no turn-by-turn). Separately, Strava reads Apple-Workout-app activities from HealthKit (last 30 days, native Workout app recordings only — third-party-written workouts are ignored to avoid duplicates) and writes back only type/distance/duration/calories/route — it does NOT write heart rate, HRV, resting HR, or sleep to HealthKit.
- Gamification layer (relevant to the RPG goal): monthly challenges with auto-tracked progress (distance/elevation/activity-count) and completion badges in a Trophy Case; segment achievements (KOM/QOM crowns, top-10 medals, PR cups); Local Legend for most repeats of a segment in a rolling 90 days; Group Challenges for up to 24 friends with custom goals; weekly progress/consistency framing rather than daily streaks. Strava is repeatedly cited alongside Duolingo as a top gamification case study, but it deliberately has no XP/levels — an actual leveling system is open space.
- What users love (from reviews/research): logging + progress tracking are the top motives (83%/78%), then peer comparison (31%); segments turn any route into an async race; Matched Runs auto-benchmark repeated routes; Beacon safety sharing (live location to up to 3 contacts, free when phone-recorded, paid from watch/device); privacy controls (hide start/end up to 1 mile, hide map, hide stats). What users hate: paywall creep, subscription price hikes, GPS/auto-pause failures (activities recording 0.0 mi), battery drain, social-comparison pressure/addiction, and AI summaries (Athlete Intelligence) seen as low-value.
- Athlete Intelligence (Oct 2024, subscriber-only beta) generates an LLM text summary after each upload, aggregates 30-day trends across pace/HR/elevation/power/Relative Effort, and surfaces milestones (fastest pace, longest distance, biggest climb); reviewers found it pleasant but not worth paying for — a caution against over-investing in AI summaries.

## Specifics
- Free tier: GPS record/upload unlimited, social feed, kudos, comments, clubs, individual challenges, Beacon (phone recording only), segment matching + top-10 leaderboard view, basic stats, dark mode (free for all, options: always dark / always light / match system)
- Paid ($11.99/mo, $79.99/yr): full + filtered segment leaderboards (by age, weight, date, followers), Live Segments (real-time vs PR/KOM during activity), route builder with global-heatmap overlay + suggested routes + offline maps, Personal Heatmap, Night Heatmap (activities between sunset and sunrise, past 1 yr), Weekly Heatmap, Fitness & Freshness chart, Relative Effort + weekly RE chart, Best Efforts trends, Matched Runs/Rides, 3D Flyover, Group Challenges (<=24 people, custom goal), goals (custom time/distance/segment goals), training log, workout analysis (laps/splits/zone distribution), Athlete Intelligence, Year in Sport (paywalled Dec 2025), Beacon from devices (Garmin/Apple Watch), race Performance Predictions
- Relative Effort = TRIMP-style sum over HR zones: score = sum(time_in_zone_i * coefficient_i) with coefficients increasing steeply by zone; zones derived from user max HR (entered or estimated); falls back to Perceived Exertion (1-10 slider) when no HR; shown with weekly-average comparison band ('typical range')
- Fitness & Freshness: impulse-response (Banister) model; input = daily Training Load (power) or Relative Effort (HR); Fitness = long-decay exponential accumulation (~6 weeks), Fatigue = short-decay (~1 week), Form = Fitness - Fatigue; rendered as 3-line time-series chart with activity dots
- Best Efforts running distances: 400m, 1/2 mile, 1K, 1 mile, 2 mile, 5K, 10K, 15K, 10 mile, 20K, half marathon, 30K, marathon, 50K; uses elapsed time not moving time; tracks top-3 lifetime + top-10 annual per distance; achievements shown as PR/2nd/3rd medals on the activity
- Segment mechanics: user-created start/end GPS gates; every upload auto-matched; leaderboards overall/year/today with filters (paid); KOM/QOM/Course Record crowns; Local Legend = most efforts on segment in trailing 90 days regardless of speed
- Map Types (#statmaps) polyline coloring: pace/speed = darker blue faster; HR = darker red higher; elevation = yellow(low)->black(high); gradient = yellow descent / red incline (GPS cross-referenced to elevation DB); power = darker purple; also time, surface type, temperature, 3D terrain; changed via Edit Activity > Change Map Type
- Flyover: animated 3D camera flight along route polyline over terrain, powered by FATMAP (acquired 2023), subscriber-only, available on activities, routes, and segments
- HealthKit read behavior: imports only workouts recorded by Apple's native Workout app (HKWorkout source check), 30-day historical window, ignores workouts written to Health by other 3rd-party apps to prevent duplicate/looping syncs
- HealthKit write behavior: Strava writes HKWorkout with activity type, distance, duration, active calories, and route (HKWorkoutRoute) only — no heart rate samples, no HRV, no resting HR, no sleep
- Strava watchOS app: standalone recording (no phone needed), redesigned Sept 2025 (watchOS 10+) with Live Segments on wrist; route navigation beta Jan 2026 (visual breadcrumb track, no turn-by-turn alerts yet, laggy map rendering reported); criticized as fewer data fields/customization than Apple Workout or WorkOutDoors
- Beacon: live location URL shared with up to 3 contacts via text; free when recording on phone; device-initiated Beacon (Apple Watch/Garmin) requires subscription
- Privacy: 'Edit Map Visibility' hides up to 1 mile (options 200m/400m/etc.) around start/end or hides whole map; per-activity or global; can hide pace/power/calories/HR from others; activity visibility levels: Everyone / Followers / Only You
- Auto-pause: speed-threshold based (running <~1 mph, cycling uses GPS speed); notorious failure mode when GPS is poor -> activities logged at 0.0 mi; users often disable it
- Gamification inventory: monthly challenges (distance/elevation/count/single-activity), completion badges stored in Trophy Case, segment crowns/medals, PR cups on activities, Local Legend laurel, Group Challenges, weekly snapshot + year-over-year stats; NO XP, NO levels, NO avatar — Strava's gamification is social-comparison-based, not RPG-based
- Athlete Intelligence: post-upload AI text summary + 30-day trend analysis (pace/HR/elevation/power/RE) + milestone detection (fastest pace, longest run, biggest climb); public beta, 14 languages, subscriber-only
- Matched Runs/Rides: auto-groups activities on essentially the same route and renders a small trend chart of times across attempts — benchmark-against-yourself feature, subscriber-only
- Strava brand: signature orange #FC4C02 used for primary actions/brand marks; feed cards = white cards, big route map thumbnail, 3-stat row (Distance / Pace or Elev Gain / Time) — a proven minimal activity-card layout worth adapting to cream/coral palette
- Nov 2024 API terms: third-party apps may only display a user's data back to that user (killed coach-view and leaderboard apps); from June 30 2026 standard API access requires an active Strava subscription
- Kudos research (ScienceDirect 2022): receiving kudos causally increases run frequency; users mirror behavior of kudos-friends — social reinforcement is the retention engine
- Free-tier leaderboard limitation since May 2020: non-subscribers see only top 10 (plus their own placement), cannot filter
- Strava tracks cycling Best Efforts power curve (5s to duration) and elevation records (Biggest Climb, single-ride Elevation Gain)

## Recommendations
- Copy Best Efforts wholesale: auto-scan each run's GPS/time stream for fastest elapsed-time windows at 1K/1mi/5K/10K/half and keep top-3 lifetime + top-10 yearly per distance. It needs no server, no other users, and is Strava's highest-value single-player analytics feature — and it maps perfectly onto RPG 'record' achievements and XP bonuses.
- Implement Fitness & Freshness locally: compute a daily TRIMP from HealthKit HR samples (sum time-in-zone x zone coefficient, zones from user max HR), then Fitness = EWMA(42d), Fatigue = EWMA(7d), Form = difference. This gives you Strava-premium-grade training-load and recovery curves for free, and Form is a natural input to the avatar's displayed 'energy/condition' state.
- For route maps, go beyond a plain polyline: render MapKit MKPolyline with per-segment gradient coloring by pace or HR (Strava's most-loved map feature), and build a personal heatmap by overlaying all stored HKWorkoutRoute polylines with low-alpha strokes. Recolor Strava's schemes into the Claude palette (e.g. coral #D97757 intensity ramp on cream instead of blue/red).
- Being a personal HealthKit-native app is a structural advantage over Strava: read HR, HRV, resting HR, sleep, and workouts from ANY source directly (Strava can't write HR to Health and only imports native-Workout-app activities). Record via HKWorkoutSession on the Watch or just ingest Ultra 3 workouts from the native Workout app — the latter is far less work and avoids Strava's 'basic watch app' criticism.
- Skip Strava's social layer but keep its reward grammar in single-player form: PR 'medals' on activity cards, monthly auto-tracked challenges, a trophy case, and a Local-Legend-style consistency award — then add the XP/leveling/avatar layer Strava conspicuously lacks (convert Relative Effort or distance into XP so harder efforts level the character faster).
- Adopt the Strava feed-card layout as the home screen unit: rounded card, route-map thumbnail with gradient polyline, title, and a 3-stat row (distance / pace / time) — it is the proven minimal presentation and translates directly to a cream background with terracotta accents and generous whitespace.
- Everything Strava paywalls ($80/yr) is computable on-device from HealthKit data: leader-less segments (personal matched-routes with attempt-trend charts), F&F curves, heatmaps, best efforts, year-in-review. Positioning the personal app as 'Strava Premium analytics without the paywall or social pressure' is the clearest product thesis — users' top complaints are paywall creep and comparison anxiety, both of which a private app eliminates by design.
- Learn from Strava's misses: make auto-pause conservative or manual (top complaint is corrupted 0.0-mile activities), keep any AI/text summary minimal or skip it (Athlete Intelligence is rated 'not worth paying for'), and use elapsed-vs-moving time correctly (store both; use elapsed for best efforts, moving for pace stats).
