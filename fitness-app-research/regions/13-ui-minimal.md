# ui-minimal

## Key findings
- Anthropic's official brand palette (from the brand-guidelines skill) is: Dark #141413, Light #FAF9F5, Mid Gray #B0AEA5, Light Gray #E8E6DC, Orange accent #D97757, Blue accent #6A9BCC, Green accent #788C5D — headings in a geometric sans (Poppins as stand-in), body in a serif (Lora as stand-in).
- Anthropic.com's live CSS tokens confirm #F0EEE6 is named 'color-ivory-medium' and #D97757 is named 'color-clay'; the site declares 15 accent swatches including 8 muted accents (clay, fig, cactus, sky, heather, olive, manilla, kraft) used to color-code content categories — a ready-made scheme for color-coding fitness domains (sleep, nutrition, workouts, recovery).
- Anthropic's real web fonts are Tiempos (serif body/display) and Styrene A/B (sans headlines/UI), self-hosted and renamed 'Anthropic Serif/Sans/Mono'; the newer marketing display serif is Copernicus. Display sizes always use weight 400 (never bold) with negative letter-spacing (-0.3 to -1.5px) — the serif/sans pairing is described as the 'literary publication' voice that differentiates the brand.
- A detailed extracted Claude design-token file (VoltAgent awesome-design-md) documents the full system: cream canvas #FAF9F5, soft surface #F5F0E8, card surface #EFE9DE, warm dark surface #181715 with elevated dark #252320, ink #141413, body #3D3D3A, muted #6C6A64, hairline #E6DFD8, coral primary #CC785C with pressed state #A9583E; semantic success #5DB872, warning #D4A017, error #C64545.
- Claude's elevation philosophy is 'color-block first, shadow rare': depth comes from cream-vs-dark surface contrast and hairline borders, not drop shadows; coral is reserved for primary CTAs and rare full-bleed callout moments ('the coral IS the voltage'), never scattered.
- Claude's dark surfaces are warm near-blacks (#181715, #1F1E1B, #252320) with cream-tinted text #FAF9F5 and soft secondary #A09D96 — the dark mode rule is warm dark, never pure black or cool gray.
- Anthropic's illustration style is simple hand-drawn line-art with coral + dark strokes on cream, abstract geometric thumbnails, perfect-circle avatars, and zero photorealism — this maps directly onto a flat, line-art anime avatar for the RPG layer.
- Gentler Streak (Apple Design Award winner) replaces fixed goals with an 'Activity Path' — a moving optimal-range band computed from recent load; the UI tells you if you're in, above, or below range, making rest a first-class state instead of a broken streak.
- Gentler Streak's UX-writing tone is deliberately non-judgmental: neutral framing like 'Below Typical Thursday' instead of 'underperforming'; reviewers credit the calm tone + soft palette as the core of its 'gentle' feel — copy is a design token, not an afterthought.
- Apple HIG for Activity rings is strict: use rings only to display real Move/Exercise/Stand progress, never recolor/filter/restyle them, never use ring visuals for branding, other metrics, or decoration — so a custom app should either embed the genuine ActivityRingView or invent its own distinct progress metaphor (arcs, bands, paths), not imitate the three rings.
- Gyroscope's dashboard pattern: every daily card focuses on a single data point (square cards, customizable), weekly report cards carry the dense multi-metric views, and content is split into intuitive sections (by parts of the body) — one-metric-per-card plus richer periodic summaries is the proven minimal-dashboard recipe.
- Bevel is the current benchmark indie 'Apple-esque' all-in-one health app: it records nothing itself, acting purely as an interpretation layer over Apple Watch/HealthKit data, and turns raw metrics into a few scores (sleep, recovery, strain) with a clean, minimal, highly responsive UI — exactly the architecture a personal HealthKit app should copy.
- Calm-UX analysis of health apps: generous padding and whitespace ('the silence between the notes'), muted/pastel palettes, quiet typography, frosted blur to foreground core metrics, and progressive disclosure (collapse/expand, summary-then-detail) are the recurring techniques; large headers with small quiet subheads make typography itself the hierarchy.
- Swift Charts (iOS 16+) covers all needed minimal chart styles natively with 7 marks (Bar, Line, Area, Point, Rule, Rectangle, Sector); sparkline style = LineMark + gradient AreaMark with axes hidden; weekly bar+trend = BarMark with a RuleMark average line; SectorMark handles rings/donuts — no third-party chart library needed.
- iOS 17's SwiftUI .sensoryFeedback modifier provides semantic haptics (.success, .warning, .error, .selection, .increase, .decrease, .start, .stop, .levelChange, .impact with weight/intensity) triggered by value changes; best practice is to use semantic styles for their intended meaning, keep haptics sparse, and offer a toggle to disable.
- SF Symbols (5,000+ glyphs, SF Symbols 4/5 era, iOS 16/17+) includes the full workout figure set (figure.run, figure.walk, figure.strengthtraining.traditional, figure.outdoor.cycle, figure.pool.swim, figure.yoga, etc.) plus flame.fill, bed.double.fill, fork.knife, heart.fill, trophy.fill; symbol animations (bounce, pulse, scale) arrived with SF Symbols 5 for micro-delight without custom animation code.
- Anthropic's spacing/radius system: spacing steps 4/8/12/16/24/32/48/96px; radii 4/6/8/12/16px + pill; buttons 40px tall with 8px radius; cards 12px radius with 32px padding (16-20px is the mobile-appropriate equivalent); focus states use a 3px coral ring at 15% alpha.
- Surface pacing is a Claude signature: alternate cream canvas, cream card, and dark blocks (never the same surface twice in a row) — in an app this translates to a cream dashboard with occasional dark 'feature' cards (e.g., the workout-in-progress card or the avatar/RPG card) for rhythm.

## Specifics
- Light palette tokens: canvas #FAF9F5; grouped/alt background #F0EEE6 (ivory-medium); card #FFFFFF or #F5F0E8; emphasized card #EFE9DE; hairline border #E6DFD8; ink #141413; body #3D3D3A; secondary #6C6A64; tertiary/disabled #B0AEA5.
- Accent tokens: primary coral/clay #D97757 (Anthropic official accent; marketing variant #CC785C, pressed #A9583E); blue #6A9BCC; green #788C5D; amber #E8A55A; teal #5DB8A6; semantic success #5DB872, warning #D4A017, error #C64545.
- Suggested domain color mapping using Anthropic's muted accent logic: workouts/energy = clay #D97757, sleep = sky/blue #6A9BCC, recovery/readiness = olive/green #788C5D, nutrition/calories = amber #E8A55A or manilla, heart = a muted fig/heather tone.
- Dark palette tokens (warm dark, from Anthropic dark surfaces): background #181715, secondary background #1F1E1B, elevated card #252320, text #FAF9F5, secondary text #A09D96, hairline = white at ~8% alpha, keep coral #D97757 as accent (it passes contrast on #181715).
- Typography: display = serif, weight 400 only, negative tracking (SwiftUI: .fontDesign(.serif) gives New York as a free Tiempos/Copernicus stand-in); body/UI = SF Pro (stand-in for Styrene); numerals in data displays should use .monospacedDigit().
- Type scale mapped to iOS: display 34pt serif regular (largeTitle), section head 28pt serif, card title 17pt sans medium, metric numeral 28-40pt (serif or SF rounded, monospaced digits), body 16-17pt, caption 13pt, uppercase micro-label 11-12pt with +1.5px letter-spacing (Anthropic caption-uppercase pattern).
- Spacing scale: 4/8/12/16/24/32/48; screen horizontal margin 20pt; card internal padding 16-20pt; inter-card gap 12-16pt; section gap 32-48pt.
- Radius scale: 12pt for standard cards, 16pt for hero/feature cards, 8pt for buttons/inputs (40pt tall), pill (capsule) for badges/tags; use continuous corner curvature (.rect(cornerRadius:style:.continuous)).
- Elevation rule: no drop shadows by default; hierarchy via surface color steps (canvas -> card -> emphasized card -> dark card) plus 1px hairline #E6DFD8; at most one soft shadow on a floating action element.
- Focus/selection style: 3px coral ring at 15% alpha (Anthropic text-input-focused token).
- Gentler Streak 'Activity Path' pattern: daily goal is a moving band (optimal activity range derived from rolling training load), rendered as a flowing area chart with today's point on it; states are in-range / above / below, and rest days sit inside the band rather than breaking a streak.
- Gentler Streak copy tone spec: neutral comparisons ('Below Typical Thursday'), no shame words, rest framed as productive; premium 'Go Gentler' suggests lighter workouts when load is high.
- Gyroscope card pattern: daily cards = one metric each (big numeral + tiny label + optional sparkline); weekly report = dense multi-metric card; dashboard sections grouped semantically (Gyroscope uses body parts; equivalent here: Move / Fuel / Sleep / Recover / Level).
- Bevel pattern: pure HealthKit interpretation layer (writes nothing), three headline scores (sleep / recovery / strain-like), 'Apple-esque' minimal UI, iOS 18+ only, smart alarm from sleep cycles.
- Apple HIG Activity rings rules: only real Move/Exercise/Stand data, never recolored, filtered, opacity-modified, or used in icons/branding/backgrounds; embed via HealthKitUI's ActivityRingView if showing rings at all.
- Chart spec - sparkline: Swift Charts LineMark with .interpolationMethod(.catmullRom) + AreaMark filled with LinearGradient(accent.opacity(0.25) -> .clear), .chartXAxis(.hidden), .chartYAxis(.hidden), height ~40-56pt inside metric cards.
- Chart spec - weekly bar+trend: BarMark (7 days, 6-8pt corner radius on bars, muted fill with today in accent) + RuleMark(y: average) in dashed ink with a small serif annotation label; hide gridlines, keep only sparse Y labels.
- Chart spec - progress: SectorMark (donut) or custom Circle().trim arc stroked in coral #D97757 on track #E8E6DC, round line caps — visually distinct from Apple's triple rings to respect HIG.
- SF Symbols to use: figure.run, figure.walk, figure.outdoor.cycle, figure.strengthtraining.traditional, figure.pool.swim, figure.yoga, figure.mind.and.body, flame.fill (calories), fork.knife (nutrition), bed.double.fill / moon.zzz.fill (sleep), heart.fill / bolt.heart (recovery/HRV), map.fill / location.fill (routes), trophy.fill / star.fill (RPG), chart.line.uptrend.xyaxis (trends); render in .hierarchical mode tinted with the domain accent.
- SF Symbols 5 (iOS 17) symbol effects for micro-delight: .symbolEffect(.bounce) on goal completion, .pulse on live workout indicator — replaces custom animation for gamification feedback.
- Haptics spec (iOS 17 .sensoryFeedback): .start/.stop for workout begin/end, .increase/.decrease when logging adjustments, .selection for pickers, .success for goal hit and level-up (pair with .levelChange), .impact(weight:.light) for card interactions; provide a settings toggle; never fire haptics on passive scroll.
- Progressive disclosure structure: Level 1 dashboard = 3-5 cards each with one number + sparkline; Level 2 = tap card -> full-screen detail with large chart, time-range segmented control, and stats grid; Level 3 = raw history list. Never show more than ~5 numbers on the first screen.
- Empty-state pattern (calm-tech consensus): no blank grids or gray placeholder charts — a short serif sentence in warm tone ('No workouts yet this week. Your body might be telling you something.') + one coral CTA + optional small line-art illustration in coral/ink on cream.
- Surface pacing pattern: cream canvas with cream cards, punctuated by exactly one dark (#181715) feature card per screen (e.g., active-workout card or the avatar/level card) and coral reserved for a single CTA or the single most important number.
- Anthropic illustration style for the avatar/RPG layer: flat line-art, coral + ink strokes on cream, geometric shapes, no gradients or photorealism; avatars as perfect circles; badges as pill shapes with caption-uppercase labels.
- Anthropic responsive/width principle: max content width with generous margins; on iPhone this means a single-column card stack, full-bleed nothing except the map view.
- Buttons: primary = coral fill #D97757, white text, 40pt height, 8pt radius, 14pt medium sans; secondary = canvas fill + hairline border + ink text; text-link style for tertiary actions.
- Font sourcing note: Tiempos/Styrene/Copernicus are licensed and not usable; free equivalents = New York (built-in iOS serif via .fontDesign(.serif)) or bundled Lora (Google Fonts, matches the brand-guidelines skill), with SF Pro (or bundled Poppins for headings) as sans — bundling custom fonts works fine in a sideloaded Xcode app.

## Recommendations
- Adopt the two-surface warm palette exactly: light mode canvas #FAF9F5 with grouped background #F0EEE6, cards #FFFFFF/#F5F0E8, ink #141413, hairline #E6DFD8; dark mode #181715 canvas with #252320 cards and #FAF9F5 text — never pure white, pure black, or cool gray anywhere.
- Treat coral #D97757 as scarce: one primary CTA and/or one hero metric per screen; color-code the four domains with the muted secondary accents (blue #6A9BCC sleep, green #788C5D recovery, amber #E8A55A nutrition, coral workouts) so the dashboard stays calm but scannable.
- Use serif display (New York via .fontDesign(.serif), regular weight, tight tracking) for screen titles and big metric numerals, SF Pro for labels and data, monospaced digits for anything that ticks — this single typographic move delivers most of the 'Claude feel'.
- Structure the home screen as Gyroscope-style one-metric cards (big numeral + micro-label + 40-56pt axis-less sparkline) with progressive disclosure to full-chart detail screens; cap the first screen at ~5 numbers.
- Steal Gentler Streak's moving optimal-range band for the recovery/training-load view instead of fixed streaks, and adopt its non-judgmental copy tone throughout (rest is a state, not a failure) — this pairs naturally with the RPG layer (rest days still earn recovery XP).
- Build all charts with Swift Charts only: hidden-axis sparklines (LineMark+AreaMark gradient), weekly BarMark with dashed RuleMark average, and custom trimmed-circle coral arcs for goals; do NOT imitate Apple's triple activity rings (HIG violation) — embed the real ActivityRingView if rings are wanted.
- Use flat hairline-bordered cards with 12pt continuous radius and zero shadows ('color-block first'), 20pt screen margins, 4/8/12/16/24/32/48 spacing scale, and exactly one dark #181715 feature card per screen (active workout or avatar/level card) for rhythm.
- Wire iOS 17 .sensoryFeedback semantic haptics (.start/.stop for workouts, .success + SF Symbol .bounce effect for goal-hit and level-up, .selection for pickers) and keep the avatar/RPG art in Anthropic's flat line-art style: coral+ink strokes on cream, geometric, no gradients.
